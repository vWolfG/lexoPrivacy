# Lexo Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Lexo handles information when you use the iPhone app.

## Vocabulary and study data

Lexo stores your vocabulary, translations, dictionaries, study progress, and preferences on your device so you can save words and practice them. Lexo does not send this data to the developer. Lexo does not use advertising or tracking services.

## Camera and text recognition

If you grant camera access, you can scan printed text to add words to your vocabulary. Text recognition runs on your device. Lexo does not upload scanned images or recognized text. You can revoke camera access in your iPhone’s Settings.

## Reminders and widgets

If you enable study reminders, Lexo schedules notifications on your device. You can turn them off in Lexo or in your iPhone’s Settings. The Lexo widget uses locally shared app data to display vocabulary on your Home Screen.

## Exporting vocabulary

If you export your vocabulary, you choose where to save it or which app to share it with. The service you choose may handle the exported file under its own privacy policy.

## Data retention and deletion

Your vocabulary and study data stay on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Files you export are stored separately. You can delete those files from the location where you saved them.

## Contact

For questions about this policy or how Lexo handles your data, contact the developer on Telegram at [@ronikavego](https://t.me/ronikavego). If you send a message, the developer uses the information you provide to respond to your request. Telegram handles your message under its own privacy policy.
